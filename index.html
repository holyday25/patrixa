FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about PATRIXA and ASM.

How is ASM different from a vulnerability assessment?

Traditional assessments usually start with systems you already know. ASM continuously discovers internet-facing assets from an external perspective and tracks how that exposure changes over time.

What types of assets can PATRIXA assess?

Starting with your primary domains, PATRIXA focuses on externally visible assets such as subdomains, websites, public IP addresses, and certificates. The exact scope is agreed during onboarding.

Will an assessment affect our production services?

Service stability is a priority. Assessments are designed for authorized targets and an agreed scope. Timing and assessment intensity can be coordinated for your environment.

Can teams without dedicated security staff use the results?

Yes. Risk level, impact, and recommended action are presented in clear language so both IT practitioners and business leaders can understand the overall posture.

Is this a one-time assessment?

External attack surfaces change continuously, so recurring assessment is recommended. Monitoring frequency and service scope can be tailored to your environment and needs.
